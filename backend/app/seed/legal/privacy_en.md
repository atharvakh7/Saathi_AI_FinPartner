# Saathi Privacy Notice

Version {{VERSION}}

Saathi helps you understand, plan and protect your money. This notice explains in simple words what data we collect, why we use it, how long we keep it, and your rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

## What we collect

- **Mobile number**, to log you in with a one-time code.
- **Profile details you give**: name, age, gender, state, district, work, income range, household size, and answers to scheme questions (such as social category or land ownership).
- **Money entries you add**: income, expenses, debts and goals.
- **Your chats with Saathi**, and short facts Saathi remembers from them (for example, "grows onions").
- **Messages or screenshots** you send to Fraud Shield.
- **App settings**: language, notification choices and your device's notification token.

We never ask for your Aadhaar number, PAN, bank account number, card number, OTP or PIN. Please do not type them in chat.

## Voice and screenshots

Voice recordings are turned into text and deleted immediately. Screenshots are read for text and deleted immediately. We keep only the text.

## Why we use your data

- To give you personal guidance, budgets, reminders and scheme matches.
- To check messages for scam signs.
- To keep your account secure.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. Saathi's AI runs on servers we control; your chats are not sent to outside AI companies.

## Who else receives some data

- An **SMS provider** receives your mobile number to send login codes.
- **Expo's push service** receives your device token and the reminder text, only if you allow notifications.

## How long we keep it

- Login codes: 1 day.
- Scam checks: 180 days.
- Security logs: 180 days, with personal details masked.
- Everything else: until you delete it or your account. After you delete your account, all your data is permanently erased within 30 days.

## Your rights

- **See your data**: download it from Profile › Privacy & data.
- **Correct it**: edit your profile at any time.
- **Erase it**: delete remembered facts, entries, or your whole account.
- **Withdraw consent**: turn off optional permissions such as notifications. Required consents are needed to run Saathi; if you withdraw them, you can delete your account.
- **Nominate someone** to use these rights for you in case of death or incapacity, by writing to us.
- **Complain**: contact our Grievance Officer. If you are not satisfied with the reply, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.
